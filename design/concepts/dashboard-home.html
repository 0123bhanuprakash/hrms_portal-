What changed and why

  • The top answers "what now?" Your day, then everything that needs you in one list, then quick actions. Before, the top was a picture with a greeting.
  • One list for "needs you". Approvals and your own to-dos sit together, most urgent first. Each row says why it matters and has one clear button.
  • One place per number. "Waiting for you" is only the count on that list. It is no longer repeated in the hero and a card.
  • One Quick actions list. Quick actions, Admin shortcuts and Make it yours become one list. The 6 most-used are tiles. "All shortcuts" shows the rest. Every link stays.
  • Plain words and numbers. "5 of 8 days left" instead of rings. My week is a real table that screen readers can read.
  • Phone first. Same order as desktop. The check-in button sits in a bar at the bottom, in thumb reach.
  • Calm look. No sky, glass, glow or tilt. Colour is used for status and the main action only.

Targets40 px controls on desktop, 44–48 px on phone.
StatusAlways an icon and a word ("On leave", "11 min late"). Colour is extra.
MotionLively / Calm / Off and the device "reduce motion" setting. Nothing moves on its own forever.
Screen readersLandmarks: menu, main, "Your day". One h1, an h2 per block. Buttons say what they act on ("Review 3 leave requests"). Counts and toasts are announced (live region). Loading blocks are marked busy.
LinksText links are underlined, so they don't depend on colour.

Try the interactions

  • Check out: it's before 18:30, so a short "early check-out" dialog asks for a reason (as today). Esc or Cancel closes it. After that, Your day changes and a toast confirms.
  • Review / Fix / Confirm: opens the right page (here a toast says which).
  • Data switch: shows loading, empty and error states for "Needs you" and the today blocks. "Try again" reloads.
  • Coming up tabs: click, or use the arrow keys.
  • All shortcuts and This week open and close in place.

Questions for you

  1. Team today for managers. Should team leads and managers see who in their team is in, on leave or not in yet on Home (dashed box in the Manager view)? This needs a small data change.
  2. Moving cards. You liked the moving Celebrations and Kudos cards. Moving content is hard to read for some people. Keep them still (my suggestion), or keep moving with a Pause button?
  3. Sky picture and moments. The sky picture goes away. Moments (birthday, festivals) become one short line you can close. OK?
  4. Approve on Home. Do you want to approve or decline simple requests right in the "Needs you" list, without opening the page? This would be a new feature.
  5. Font. Public Sans is used for easier reading (clear letters and numbers). OK as the default? People can still pick fonts later (phase 3).
