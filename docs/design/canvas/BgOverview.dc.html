Backgrounds · system

Ambient backgrounds — one system, a different mood per page

Every page keeps its layout, components, content and behaviour exactly as today. Only the canvas behind the content changes. All pages share the same 3 layers, so the app feels like one product; the middle layer changes with the page’s purpose so pages don’t look identical.
1 · Ambient base
A very soft glow of the theme colour at the top of every page + a light vignette. Same everywhere.
2 · Page mood
One effect per page family (aurora, particles, sweep…). Never more than one.
3 · Content
Cards stay solid. Sidebar and top bar unchanged (top bar gets a light frosted blur). Text contrast is never reduced.

Rules
  • Behind content only (z-index 0, no clicks). Cards, tables and charts keep solid surfaces.
  • Slow: nothing faster than 7 s; drifts 30–90 s; ease-in-out; no flashing.
  • Soft: max 35% opacity in dark, 22% in light (sign-in a bit more).
  • Colours come from theme tokens — personal themes recolour every mood automatically; status colours stay semantic.
  • Never two effects on one page; dialogs and the sidebar have none.
Light & fast
  • Pure CSS (+ a few SVGs) — no canvas/WebGL, no libraries.
  • Only transform and opacity animate (GPU), blur is static.
  • Paused when the tab is hidden; paused while scrolling long tables.
  • Phones: particles halved, blur smaller; low-power devices get the static version.
  • Reduced motion (system or app setting) → every effect becomes a still image.
Works with what exists
  • Atmosphere moments (birthday balloons, Diwali…) play on top of Home’s aurora — the aurora dims while a moment runs.
  • Product animations, check-in pulse, kudos hearts unchanged.
  • Light & dark: every mood has both (see each board).
  • Phase 2 whole-UI themes can swap the mood set.
