When they play

Click — reactions: the emoji pops and 3 small copies float up (like the 👋 Wave today, now for all).
Hover — covers, chips, cards: the emoji does its move once.
Appear — birthday ribbon, toasts, “all caught up”: once when shown. Never loops forever.

Stay still on purpose

Status dots 🔴 🟠 🟢 🔵, arrows and symbols ↔ ▶ ⏸ ⚑, and plain icons like 💼 🏢 do not move — they carry meaning, and moving them would distract.
Built once as a shared <Emoji> piece + one CSS file, so a new emoji added later gets a move too (default: pop).
